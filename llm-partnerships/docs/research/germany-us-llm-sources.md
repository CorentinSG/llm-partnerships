## Publication decision

The public directory includes the 17 rows that are active and have a reliability score of 4 or 5. Because a website card represents one U.S. destination, aggregate rows are expanded as follows:

- DE-US-001: three cards (UConn, UC Law San Francisco, Miami).
- DE-US-002: six cards (Cardozo, UConn, UC Law San Francisco, Indiana Maurer, Lewis & Clark, Illinois).
- DE-US-010: four cards (George Washington, Pittsburgh, Chicago-Kent, Santa Clara).
- The remaining 14 qualifying rows each produce one destination card.

This produces 27 public cards across 13 German law faculties.

## Cases deliberately not presented as current opportunities

- DE-US-011, Passau–Tulane scholarship: the published 2025–2026 call and its 1 May 2025 deadline have expired; no 2026–2027 renewal is published.
- DE-US-019, Cologne–Berkeley: Berkeley publishes Cologne as a partner, but no Cologne nomination route was found; reliability score 3/5.
- DE-US-020, Potsdam–Illinois: the page is stale and does not publish a benefit, procedure, quota, or deadline; reliability score 2/5.
- Passau–Golden Gate remains in the spreadsheet’s exclusions because it is only mentioned without usable current terms.

## Interpretation safeguards

- A German nomination does not guarantee admission, a scholarship, or the maximum advertised discount.
- A tuition-free exchange semester is not a tuition-free LL.M.; the additional LL.M. study period and living costs can remain payable.
- Historical amounts and percentages are retained as dated evidence and marked for reconfirmation.
- No German faculty source guarantees eligibility for the New York, California, or another U.S. bar exam. Eligibility is determined only by the relevant bar authority after an individual review.
- Where a row cites multiple official URLs, the card links to the principal German faculty source and summarizes the bilateral caveat recorded in the sheet.
