# Switzerland–United States LL.M. sources

Verified on 2026-08-03. The source spreadsheet contained nine candidate rows. The public directory includes only the six rows rated 4 or 5 for reliability; Basel–Boston University, Lucerne–Temple, and UZH–Tulane remain excluded until the Swiss side or current bilateral terms confirm the LL.M. pathway.

## Published pathways

- UZH–Berkeley: [UZH double-degree directory](https://www.ius.uzh.ch/en/studies/study-courses/master/ma-law-doubledegree-120/doubledegree-outgoing.html), [Berkeley partner law schools](https://www.law.berkeley.edu/academics/llm/meet-our-partners/partner-law-schools/), [Berkeley LL.M. bar information](https://www.law.berkeley.edu/students/llm-academic-advising/llm-bar-information/), and [Berkeley tuition](https://www.law.berkeley.edu/admissions/financial-aid/llm-financial-aid/cost-of-attendance/).
- UZH–Cardozo: [UZH double-degree directory](https://www.ius.uzh.ch/en/studies/study-courses/master/ma-law-doubledegree-120.html) and [UZH November 2025 factsheet](https://www.ius.uzh.ch/dam/jcr:6040b735-d9e9-4324-b304-c399ba2b0acc/20251110_Cardozo_EN.pdf).
- Lucerne–Notre Dame and Lucerne–Texas: [University of Lucerne double degree](https://www.unilu.ch/studium/studienangebot/master/rechtswissenschaftliche-fakultaet/double-degree-mlawllm/), [current comparison table](https://www.unilu.ch/fileadmin/fakultaeten/rf/0_Dekanat_RF/Dok/Studienberatung/Double_Degree_RF_Tabelle_web.pdf), and [Texas Law 2026–2027 tuition](https://law.utexas.edu/master-of-laws/tuition-expenses-and-financial-aid/).
- HSG–Fletcher: [HSG double degree](https://www.unisg.ch/en/studying/programmes/master/double-degrees/mil-double-degree/), [Fletcher dual programs](https://fletcher.tufts.edu/academics/dual-exchange-programs), and [Fletcher LL.M.](https://fletcher.tufts.edu/academics/degrees-programs/master-laws-international-law).
- IHEID–Georgetown: [Georgetown joint LL.M.](https://www.law.georgetown.edu/academics/llm-degree-programs/joint-programs/llm-global-health-law-governance/) and [Geneva Graduate Institute program](https://www.graduateinstitute.ch/programmes/global-health-law-and-governance).

## Publication rules

- No U.S. bar eligibility is guaranteed; each relevant card states the documented limitation or the need for an individual evaluation.
- The Georgetown–IHEID program is included because it is a genuine joint U.S.–Swiss LL.M., but it is explicitly described as offering no Swiss-only admission advantage.
- The Fletcher program is described as a real U.S. LL.M. but not an ABA-accredited-law-school route to a U.S. bar.
- Cardozo is published with an explicit instruction to reconfirm the next cycle because UZH currently documents it but Cardozo does not publish a bilateral page.
